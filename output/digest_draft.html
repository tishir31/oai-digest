Earnings / Financials / Fundraising

OpenAI Confidentially Files S-1 IPO Prospectus with SEC, Targeting Q4 2026 Listing at Up to $1 Trillion (May 22) — OpenAI filed its confidential S-1 with the SEC, targeting a public listing between September and November 2026 at a valuation of $852B–$1T. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are co-leading the deal. The company generates ~$2B/month in revenue ($25B annualized) but reported a negative 122% non-GAAP operating margin in Q1 2026, equating to ~$6.95B in quarterly losses. CEO Altman told staff that “filing is different from being ready to go public.” The filing arrives alongside SpaceX’s own S-1 and Anthropic’s planned listing. [CNBC] (widely reported)

Product Launches & Updates

OpenAI Achieves C2PA Conformance and Integrates Google SynthID Watermarking for AI Content Provenance (May 20) — OpenAI became a C2PA Conforming Generator and integrated Google DeepMind’s SynthID invisible watermarking into images generated by ChatGPT, Codex, and the API. SynthID embeds a pixel-level signal that survives screenshots and format changes, addressing the limitation that C2PA metadata can be stripped. A public verification tool is now in preview. [OpenAI]

OpenAI Named Leader in 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Enterprise AI Coding Agents (May 22) — Gartner placed OpenAI as a Leader in its inaugural Magic Quadrant for Enterprise AI Coding Agents, recognizing Codex for agentic software development, enterprise governance, sandboxing, and flexible deployment. Codex now has 4M+ weekly users and is deployed at companies including NVIDIA. [OpenAI]

ChatGPT Launches PowerPoint Add-In for AI-Powered Slide Creation (May 22) — A new ChatGPT add-in for Microsoft PowerPoint (beta) lets users generate, edit, and update slides from notes, documents, and existing decks. The add-in integrates with Outlook, Gmail, and SharePoint and is available to all ChatGPT tiers, including Free. [Dataconomy]
Also: Winbuzzer

OpenAI Ships Codex to Mobile App and Chrome Extension (May 23) — Codex is now available on iOS, Android, and as a Chrome extension, letting developers monitor workflows, approve commands, and launch tasks remotely. A secure relay layer keeps connected machines reachable without public internet exposure. Available across all plans. [OpenAI]

OpenAI and Dell Technologies Partner to Bring Codex to Hybrid and On-Premises Environments (May 19) — OpenAI and Dell announced a multi-year collaboration to deploy Codex via the Dell AI Data Platform and Dell AI Factory, marking OpenAI’s first explicit hybrid/on-prem enterprise distribution deal. Regulatory & Policy

Jury Unanimously Dismisses All Claims in Musk v. OpenAI Lawsuit (May 18) — A nine-member advisory jury in Oakland took less than 90 minutes to unanimously dismiss all claims, finding Musk exceeded the three-year statute of limitations. Judge Gonzalez Rogers agreed and tossed the case. Musk had sought up to $150B in disgorgement, alleging OpenAI’s founders “stole a charity.” His attorney Marc Toberoff announced plans to appeal to the Ninth Circuit. The verdict removes a major legal overhang four days before OpenAI’s IPO filing. [NPR] (widely reported)

Technical Research / Model Releases

OpenAI Model Disproves 80-Year-Old Erdős Conjecture in Discrete Geometry (May 20) — An internal general-purpose reasoning model autonomously disproved a central conjecture in discrete geometry posed by Paul Erdős in 1946 (the unit distance problem). The 125-page proof, which used algebraic number theory and the Golod-Shafarevich theorem, was verified by external mathematicians who said “AI has gone beyond being just an assistant.” The result is notable because a general-purpose model, not a math-specialized system, produced it.
