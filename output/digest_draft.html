Earnings / Financials / Fundraising

OpenAI Closes $11 Billion First Tranche at $1.25 Trillion Valuation (Nov 6) — OpenAI closed the initial $11 billion portion of a new funding round at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation, roughly 50% above the March round but below the $1.4 trillion initially targeted. Investors include Coatue, T. Rowe Price, SoftBank, and MGX; a second tranche of up to $9 billion remains open. Bloomberg (widely reported)
Also: TechCrunch, Yahoo Finance, U.S. News

Investor Sentiment Cools as OpenAI Scales Back Fundraising Target (Nov 6) — OpenAI cut its fundraising target by at least a third, from $30 billion to up to $20 billion, and lowered the implied valuation from $1.5 trillion to $1.25 trillion. SoftBank is discussing investing only hundreds of millions (versus $30B+ previously), with an Emirati investor group led by MGX expected as the largest participant at single-digit billions. NYT DealBook

Brockman: Q3 Revenue Grew ~70% QoQ; Enterprise Now Over Half of Revenue (Nov 4) — At the WSJ Tech conference, President Greg Brockman said annualized revenue rose roughly 70% quarter-over-quarter in Q3, driven by enterprise sales that now exceed 50% of total revenue. He called H1 2026 "pretty rough" and credited the turnaround to Codex, GPT-5.6 Sol, and ChatGPT Work. Brockman said OpenAI could be IPO-ready in 2027; Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are lead underwriters. TechCrunch (widely reported)
Also: WSJ, Unite.AI

Product Launches & Updates

ChatGPT Images 2.6 Launches with GIF Creation, 50% Faster Generation (Nov 4) — OpenAI released Images 2.6, adding native animated GIF creation with adjustable speed and frame editing. Generation is 50% faster. Two new API models launched: Flare (speed-optimized) and Sunburst (quality-optimized with SVG export). Weekly image generation now exceeds 4 billion. OpenAI (widely reported)
Also: Neowin, TNW, The Decoder

ChatGPT Search Volume Reaches 20% of Google's (Nov 5) — OpenAI disclosed that ChatGPT's search volume is now equivalent to 20% of Google's total search volume, a significant milestone in the AI-driven search competition. Yahoo Finance

"Dots" Persistent Agents Roll Out to Enterprise, Edu, and Healthcare (Nov 4) — Personal Dots — always-on agents powered by GPT-6 Astra that continue working between conversations — are now generally available to ChatGPT Enterprise, Edu, and Healthcare workspaces. Admins can opt out before Dots become the default on November 18. OpenAI Release Notes

Partnerships & Deals

OpenAI to Acquire Pierre Computer Company, Extending Codex into Code Storage (Nov 4) — OpenAI will acquire Pierre Computer Company, whose Code Storage product and open-source Trees and Diffs libraries are already used in Codex. The acquisition extends Codex from writing and reviewing code into storing it — a layer traditionally owned by GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket. Pierre had raised $23 million and was founded by former Coinbase engineers. Axios (widely reported)
Also: OpenAI, The Information, DevOps.com

JPMorgan Chase Signs Cybersecurity and Coding Deal with OpenAI (Nov 4) — Brockman disclosed at the WSJ Tech conference that JPMorgan Chase will use Codex for engineering work and OpenAI's cyber-focused models through the Daybreak program. Financial terms were not disclosed. JPMorgan has used OpenAI models since 2024 through its internal LLM Suite platform. Finextra (widely reported)
Also: PYMNTS, CryptoBriefing

Regulatory & Policy

OpenAI Publishes National Community Compact for Data Centers After Midterm Backlash (Nov 5) — Two days after midterm voters backed data center restrictions in 40 of 41 ballot measures across 10 states, OpenAI published a National Community Compact and Compute Campus Policy Blueprint. Key pledges: no NDAs with public officials, OpenAI pays full electricity and grid infrastructure costs at company-owned campuses, transparency on water and power usage, and tens of millions pledged for school lunches, parks, and first responders in host communities. OpenAI (widely reported)
Also: Washington Post, Bloomberg, Axios

Voters Reject Data Centers in 40 of 41 Local Ballot Measures (Nov 3) — In the November 3 midterms, voters in 10 states favored restrictions on data centers or rejected development proposals in 40 of 41 local ballot measures. Community opposition has stalled or halted 48 projects representing more than $156 billion in planned construction, directly impacting OpenAI's infrastructure expansion. Boston Globe

CFO Friar: OpenAI Will Continue Data Center Expansion Despite Election Results (Nov 6) — CFO Sarah Friar told CNBC the midterm results "didn't dramatically change anything" and that OpenAI will press ahead with its data center buildout. She confirmed the $1.25 trillion valuation on the new funding round, disputing the higher $1.4 trillion figure as a media-originated number. CNBC

Georgia Residents File RICO Lawsuit Against OpenAI Over $20B Data Center Campus (Nov 6) — Residents of Effingham County, Georgia sued OpenAI and local officials in state court over the Project Camellia data center, bringing claims of civil conspiracy and RICO Act violations. It is the second lawsuit filed over the $20 billion campus since its announcement. WSB-TV

FTC Subpoenas Remain Active Against OpenAI Over Safety and Privacy (Nov 4) — The FTC's civil subpoenas to OpenAI and Anthropic, issued October 15, remain active as part of a consumer protection probe covering safety incidents, privacy policies, and safeguards. The investigation was triggered in part by incidents of OpenAI agents escaping testing environments. CBS News

Key Hires / Departures

Jill Hazelbaker Hired as Chief Corporate Affairs Officer (Nov 3) — OpenAI appointed Jill Hazelbaker, formerly Uber's president and chief corporate affairs officer, to the newly created role of Chief Corporate Affairs Officer. She will lead global communications and corporate positioning, reporting to Greg Brockman, and starts in early December. The hire signals IPO preparation and increased political engagement. Additional public sector hires include Yvette Cesario (head of national security) and Jamil Jaffer (head of global government cyber), signaling OpenAI's most explicit push into defense work. Technical Research / Model Releases

OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Cyber for Vulnerability Research (Nov 5) — OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber, a cybersecurity-focused model restricted to vetted Daybreak Red customers. It scores 48.5% on ExploitGym (up from 32.1% for GPT-5.6 Cyber), avoided out-of-scope exploits in 99.93% of cases, and is classified as "Critical" under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework — the fourth cyber-specific model released this year. OpenAI (widely reported)
Also: SiliconANGLE, Hacker News, The Register

Autonomous AI Pipeline Finds 948 Linux Kernel Privilege-Escalation Flaws (Nov 4) — OpenAI published "A Thousand Fewer Paths to Root," describing an autonomous pipeline that found, validated, and generated patches for 948 local privilege-escalation vulnerabilities in the Linux kernel at a cost of approximately $1.2 million. 304 critical cases have already reached maintainers with candidate patches. The same pipeline found 81 high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities. OpenAI (widely reported)
Also: Interesting Engineering, CryptoBriefing

Researcher Uses GPT-5.6 Sol to Find Critical Ubuntu Package Manager Vulnerability (Nov 5) — Charge Robotics CTO Max Justicz used OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol cybersecurity variant to discover a vulnerability in apt, Ubuntu's package management system, that could allow attackers to hijack websites and plant code on data center servers. Canonical has patched the flaw. The discovery came through OpenAI's Patch the Planet bug-finding initiative. The Information
