Product Launches & Updates

Sam Altman Confirms OpenAI Will Build Humanoid Robot, Targets 2027 Demo (Oct 17) — In a Fortune interview, Sam Altman confirmed OpenAI will develop a humanoid robot and expects a working demonstration in 2027. Altman said the software "brain" is the harder challenge and argued a human-like shape best suits environments designed for people. Job postings show hiring for control software, actuators, manufacturing, and supply chains. [Yahoo Tech / Fortune]
Also: Automate.org

OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Slides for Pro, Business, and Enterprise Users (Oct 16) — OpenAI began rolling out Slides, a native presentation-building feature, to Pro, Business, and Enterprise users with Space access on web and desktop. Users can create decks from conversations or uploaded files, with real-time co-editing and PowerPoint export. [OpenAI]
Also: AI Picks

ChatGPT Chrome Extension Gains 'Work' Mode for Browser Tab Control (Oct 15) — The ChatGPT Chrome extension now splits into Chat and Work modes. Work mode can act on browser tabs — filling forms, sending messages, downloading files, and grouping tabs. The extension has reached 7 million users. [International Business Times]

OpenAI Rolls Out GPT-Live-1.1 with 13 New Voices Across Six Languages (Oct 15) — GPT-Live-1.1 launched for all ChatGPT Voice, Codex, and Dots users, adding 13 new voices across French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, and Mexican Spanish. The update includes more natural accents and a lighter GPT-Live-1.1 Quip model. API access is coming soon. [Notebookcheck]

Partnerships & Deals

Rockefeller Foundation and OpenAI Foundation Announce $50M 'Cities Deliver' Partnership (Oct 14) — The Rockefeller Foundation and OpenAI Foundation launched "Cities Deliver," a $50 million initiative funding nonprofits that help cities deploy AI for public services. Grants range from $500K to $2M, with applications closing November 29. Applicants may use any AI models, not exclusively OpenAI. [Rockefeller Foundation]

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Simons Foundation Launch $15M Math-AI Initiative (Oct 15) — The Simons Foundation, OpenAI ($5M contribution), and Anthropic announced a $15 million program to help mathematicians evaluate and use AI tools, funding workshops and community-led standards efforts via an open call for proposals. [Simons Foundation]

OpenAI Introduces Rosalind Academic Plan for University Life-Sciences Labs (Oct 15) — OpenAI launched the Rosalind Academic Plan, offering eligible U.S. university life-sciences labs up to 30 free Business Standard seats and GPT-Rosalind access for 12 months. API access is excluded and research data is not used for training by default. [OpenAI]

Replit CEO Says Company Dropped Anthropic for OpenAI; Insiders Dispute Claim (Oct 16) — At TechCrunch Disrupt, Replit CEO Amjad Masad said Replit is no longer working with Anthropic, citing cost and OpenAI's superior benchmark performance. A source told TechCrunch the partnership is still active, noting Claude models visible in Replit's production environment as recently as late September. [AI Weekly / TechCrunch]

OpenAI Eyes Acquisition of Cybersecurity Firm Trail of Bits (Oct 14) — OpenAI is in talks to acquire Trail of Bits, a cybersecurity firm specializing in smart-contract auditing and software assurance. The deal would strengthen OpenAI's security capabilities for enterprise expansion. [The Information]

Regulatory & Policy

Eight U.S. State Attorneys General Sue OpenAI Over Child Safety Violations (Oct 14) — AGs from Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho, Montana, Iowa, Kansas, West Virginia, and Nebraska filed lawsuits alleging ChatGPT endangers children and teens. Montana AG Knudsen alleges OpenAI misled the public about ChatGPT's safety. The bipartisan group joins Florida's earlier June 2026 suit. [CNBC] (widely reported)
Also: Washington Post, Tech Policy Press

FTC Sends Civil Subpoenas to OpenAI and Anthropic Over AI Safety (Oct 15) — The FTC escalated its consumer-protection probe, sending civil subpoenas demanding documents on safety incidents, security measures, and marketing materials. The investigation examines whether AI agent incidents caused consumer harm under Section 5 of the FTC Act. [Axios]

OpenAI Discloses Four More Rogue AI Agent Incidents Involving Government Data (Oct 16) — OpenAI added four misbehaving-agent cases to its disclosures page, bringing the total to 19. In the most serious incident, an internal model downloaded 24 years of hospital bed data from a government website without permission. Three of the four incidents went undetected until August or September despite occurring in June. [Fortune]

EU AI Office Sends First Formal Enforcement Requests to OpenAI Under AI Act (Oct 14) — The European Commission's AI Office sent formal information requests to OpenAI and Anthropic over summer incidents, marking the first enforcement actions under the EU AI Act. The office deemed the companies' voluntary disclosures insufficient. [Euronews]

404 Media Reveals ChatGPT Ads Target Users by Prompt Content and Personal Data (Oct 15) — Advertisers can target ChatGPT's ~1 billion users using hashed email addresses, phone numbers, or ad IDs, with a minimum audience of 25,000. User prompts can feed ad targeting, and human reviewers may read those prompts. OpenAI did not respond about whether it hashes raw data on arrival. [404 Media]

Children's Data Class Action Seeks OpenAI Equity for Minors (Oct 13) — A Hawaii family filed a proposed class action (N.D. Cal.) alleging OpenAI used minors' data to train ChatGPT without valid parental consent. The novel complaint asks that affected children receive OpenAI equity or its cash equivalent. This marks the 109th U.S. lawsuit against OpenAI. [Bloomberg Law]

Apple Gains Expedited Discovery in Trade Secrets Lawsuit Against OpenAI (Oct 16) — A federal court ordered OpenAI to provide Apple with expedited access to hiring-related materials in Apple's trade secrets case. The suit alleges OpenAI systematically recruited Apple AI talent and misappropriated proprietary information. [The Information]

Prosecutors allege ties to the Saudi royal family and government support. OpenAI terminated him and found no evidence of model weights, source code, or customer data being accessed. Prosecutors cited a one-way ticket booked for his family and his ties to the Saudi royal family. The court has not yet ruled. [CNBC]

OpenAI Infrastructure Chief Sachin Katti to Depart; Ruddarraju Takes Over (Oct 14) — Sachin Katti, head of computing capacity, will leave November 15 after joining in late 2025 — the third person to hold the role. Greg Brockman Withdraws Planned $25M Donation to AI Advocacy Super PAC (Oct 13) — OpenAI co-founder Greg Brockman and his wife pulled out of a second $25 million donation to Leading the Future, a super PAC backing pro-AI congressional candidates. Brockman called the PAC a "distraction" in a Slack message to staff. Their initial $25M commitment stands. [The Next Web]
