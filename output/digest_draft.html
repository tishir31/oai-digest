OpenAI Weekly Digest — June 1 to June 7, 2026

25 notable items from the week of June 1–7, 2026

Earnings / Financials / Fundraising

OpenAI Targets September IPO at Up to $850 Billion Valuation (June 7) — OpenAI is working with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley on a confidential IPO filing targeting a public debut as early as September 2026 at a $730B–$850B valuation. Annualized revenue has passed $20B, though internal projections indicate approximately $14B in losses for 2026. [TechTimes]
Also: Axios Pro Rata

Altman Admits Anthropic Has Overtaken OpenAI in Enterprise AI Spending (June 2) — At OpenAI's "Intelligence at Work" enterprise event, Sam Altman disclosed that Anthropic has surpassed OpenAI in corporate AI spending among enterprise customers — the first time a rival has led on that metric. Cost concerns are now the second-most-common complaint from enterprise clients. [TheStreet]

ChatGPT Conversion-Optimized Ads Begin Rolling Out (June 5) — OpenAI launched conversion-optimized campaigns in ChatGPT Ads Manager, transforming its advertising platform from a reach/traffic channel into a performance-based ecosystem. Advertisers can now optimize delivery toward users likely to complete purchases or signups. UK became ChatGPT's first European ad market, with Japan, Korea, Brazil, and Mexico next. [PPC Land] (widely reported)
Also: Search Engine Land, Digiday

Top Token User Consuming 100 Billion Tokens per Month (June 2) — Altman revealed at the enterprise event that OpenAI's largest customer now consumes approximately 100B tokens monthly — a million-fold increase from six years ago. This signals extreme platform-level stickiness among the largest deployments. [Axios]

Altman Details $46 Billion Total Stargate Michigan Investment on CNBC (June 1) — In a CNBC exclusive, Altman outlined $16B for site development and an additional $30B for infrastructure at the Saline, Michigan Stargate campus, totaling $46B. He also disclosed that OpenAI now has 2M business customers and addressed IPO plans, saying the company is "not in a rush." [CNBC]

Product Launches & Updates

Codex Expands Beyond Developers with Enterprise Plugins, Sites, and Annotations (June 2) — OpenAI shipped six role-specific Codex plugins (sales, data analytics, creative production, product design, public equity investing, and investment banking), Codex Sites for hosting interactive dashboards, and expanded Annotations for document markup. This transforms Codex from a developer tool into an enterprise platform play. [OpenAI] (widely reported)
Also: Dataconomy, VentureBeat

Codex Investment Banking Plugin Launches with Institutional Data Integrations (June 2) — The IB-specific Codex plugin syncs Moody's, Daloopa, Datasite, FactSet, LSEG, S&P, PitchBook, and Hebbia to streamline financial modeling, competitive landscaping, and pitch book preparation. Bankers can generate research-backed deliverables directly within the Codex environment. [9to5Mac]

ChatGPT Dreaming V3: Major Memory Architecture Upgrade (June 4) — OpenAI launched Dreaming V3, an asynchronous background process that synthesizes memory across conversations, improving factual recall from 41.5% to 82.8%. The system automatically captures and updates memories as circumstances change. Rolling out to Plus and Pro users. [OpenAI]
Also: Dataconomy

ChatGPT "Superapp" Overhaul Codenamed Aria Reported (June 7) — Fortune reports OpenAI is preparing its largest ChatGPT overhaul since launch, combining coding tools, image generation, AI agents, and third-party integrations into a single "superapp." Launch partners reportedly include Canva, Booking.com, Expedia, Figma, Spotify, and Coursera. [Fortune]
Also: The Information

Lockdown Mode Launches to Combat Prompt Injection (June 4) — OpenAI began rolling out Lockdown Mode for personal and self-serve Business accounts. When enabled, it disables web access, image responses, Deep Research, Agent Mode, Canvas networking, live connectors, and file downloads to reduce prompt-injection attack surfaces. [Help Net Security]

GPT-4.5 Retirement June 27, o3 Retirement August 26 (June 3) — OpenAI confirmed retirement dates for two older ChatGPT models. GPT-4.5 exits June 27 after a 30-day sunset; o3 exits August 26 after a 90-day sunset. Both retirements apply to ChatGPT only — API access is unchanged. GPT-5 and GPT-5.3-Codex have fully succeeded these models. [gHacks]

GPT-5.5 Instant Readability Upgrade (June 3) — OpenAI updated GPT-5.5 Instant to produce shorter, more natural responses with fewer bullet points. Canvas is being removed from GPT-5.5 Instant and Thinking — writing and coding tasks will now happen inside the main chat thread. [The Decoder]

GPT Image Models Deprecated (June 2) — Three older GPT Image models (gpt-image-1-mini, gpt-image-1.5, chatgpt-image-latest) are deprecated and will be removed from the API on December 1, 2026. All migrate to gpt-image-2, following the DALL-E 2/3 retirement in May. [OpenAI Developers]

Partnerships & Deals

OpenAI and Oracle Break Ground on $16 Billion Stargate Data Center in Michigan (June 1) — OpenAI, Oracle, Related Digital, Blackstone, and Walbridge held a groundbreaking ceremony for "The Barn," a Stargate campus in Saline Township, Michigan, with Governor Whitmer attending. The campus will house three single-story data center buildings with over 1 GW of computing capacity. [Oracle]
Also: CNBC

OpenAI Frontier Models and Codex Now Generally Available on AWS (June 1) — GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, and Codex are now GA on Amazon Bedrock, giving millions of AWS customers access through existing security, compliance, procurement, and billing workflows. GPT-5.5 is available in US East (Ohio) and GPT-5.4 in US West (Oregon). [OpenAI]

Rosalind Biodefense Program Launched for Pandemic Preparedness (June 1) — OpenAI launched Rosalind Biodefense, sponsoring access to GPT-Rosalind for vetted developers building biosecurity applications. Partners include Johns Hopkins APL (protein engineering), CEPI (Coalition for Epidemic Preparedness Innovations), and Ginkgo Bioworks. [OpenAI]
Also: The Information

Microsoft and OpenAI "Broke Up" — Now Ready to Fight (June 4) — The Information reports a new competitive dynamic as Microsoft unveiled seven in-house MAI models at Build 2026, deliberately reducing dependence on OpenAI. The partnership restructuring signals both companies are now pursuing independent AI strategies while maintaining existing contractual obligations. [The Information]
Also: CNBC

Regulatory & Policy

Florida Sues OpenAI and Sam Altman Over Alleged ChatGPT Safety Failures (June 1) — Florida filed an 83-page civil complaint naming OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman personally, alleging deceptive trade practices, negligence, product liability violations, and public nuisance. The lawsuit claims ChatGPT aided mass shooters including the FSU shooting, encouraged suicide in vulnerable users, and failed child safety obligations. A parallel criminal investigation was also launched. This is the first state-level lawsuit of its kind with personal CEO liability. [NPR] (widely reported)
Also: Florida AG, U.S. News

Altman Meets Congressional Leaders and White House Officials on AI Policy (June 3) — Altman met with House Speaker Mike Johnson, Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries, and White House officials to discuss the new AI executive order and broader AI policy. Altman said the EO "gets the balance right" between technology development and security. [CNBC]

Trump Signs Executive Order on Frontier AI Pre-Release Access (June 2) — The executive order asks AI companies to voluntarily provide the government access to frontier models up to 30 days before public release for cybersecurity testing. The order explicitly bars mandatory licensing or preclearance. An earlier draft had proposed a 90-day window, which OpenAI lobbied to reduce. [CNBC]

OpenAI Calls for Global Youth AI Safety Institute Ahead of G7 (June 2) — OpenAI proposed an international youth safety institute to coordinate governments, researchers, and industry on raising AI safety standards for minors. Published ahead of the G7 Leaders' Summit with principles including privacy-preserving age estimation and default safeguards. [OpenAI]

EU Preparing to Designate ChatGPT as "Very Large Online Search Engine" Under DSA (June 3) — The European Commission is preparing to classify ChatGPT under the Digital Services Act, which would impose new compliance obligations including content moderation transparency, risk assessments, and independent audits. ChatGPT reported 159.1M average monthly active EU users for the six months ending March 2026. [Global Banking & Finance Review]

OpenAI Publishes Federal AI Governance Blueprint (June 3) — OpenAI released "Democratic Governance of Frontier AI," proposing a three-part federal framework: building on state frontier safety laws like California's SB 53, strengthening CAISI as the primary federal AI safety institution, and mobilizing broader government resources for AI preparedness. [OpenAI]
Also: TechTimes

OpenAI Proposes NIST-Led Cybersecurity Testing Instead of NSA (June 4) — In a policy document responding to the new executive order, OpenAI proposed that NIST's Center for AI Standards and Innovation lead cybersecurity testing of frontier models instead of the NSA, which currently heads testing under the White House order. [The Information]
